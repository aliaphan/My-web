01Equity research

Meta Platforms (META)

Valuation lead and presenter, 4-person team · BUSN 334 class project · Sep 2026

What I did

Built the leverage analysis, DCF (with WACC, free cash flow, and working capital forecasts), and peer multiples valuation. I presented the valuation and our recommendation in the team's equity research presentation.

Result

SELL recommendation with a $530 target price, 28% below the $738.79 share price as of Sep 10, 2026. The target blends the DCF ($269.50 per share) and multiples ($789.96 per share) 50/50. Our thesis: about $137B of planned 2026 AI capex pushes free cash flow below zero, so the price assumes the AI bet pays off fast.

Cancellation of Debt Income

Author · Tax Accounting · Nov 2025
